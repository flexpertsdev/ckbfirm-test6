## 📝 NOTES FOR AI BUILDER

### Voice & Tone Guidelines
**When writing/editing copy, maintain these rules:**
- [Voice characteristic 1 - e.g., "Conversational but professional"]
- [Voice characteristic 2 - e.g., "Use 'you' and 'we' language"]
- [Voice characteristic 3 - e.g., "Be specific with numbers and examples"]
- [Voice characteristic 4 - e.g., "Avoid jargon unless defining it"]
- [Voice characteristic 5 - e.g., "Keep sentences under 25 words"]

### Content Formatting Rules
- **Headlines:** 6-10 words maximum
- **Body copy:** 15-20 words per sentence average
- **Feature descriptions:** 20-30 words
- **Testimonials:** 25-50 words
- **Paragraphs:** 3-4 sentences maximum

### SEO Implementation Rules
- Include target keyword in H1
- Use keyword variations in H2s
- Add internal links to related pages (2-4 per page)
- Alt text should describe image content, not stuff keywords
- Meta descriptions should be compelling, not just keyword lists

### Accessibility Requirements
- Every image needs descriptive alt text
- Every form field needs a label
- Every button needs descriptive text (no "Click here")
- Color contrast must pass WCAG AA (4.5:1)
- Headings must follow logical hierarchy (h1 → h2 → h3)
